Tour overview

Your sacred journey at a glance: from Adiyogi at the foot of the Velliangiri hills, through twelve of Tamil Nadu’s great temples, to the shore temples of Mahabalipuram and the temple town of Kanchipuram.

Tamil Nadu’s temples are living places of worship, with rituals that have continued for more than a thousand years. Each day is planned around darshan times, with comfortable stays, all meals and a private vehicle throughout.

AdiyogiThe 112-ft Shiva face and Dhyanalinga at the Isha Yoga Center
SrirangamSri Ranganathaswamy, one of the largest functioning temple complexes in the world
ThanjavurBrihadeeswarar, the great Chola temple and UNESCO World Heritage site
MahabalipuramPallava-era Shore Temple and rock-cut monuments

Good to know

Dress code

Traditional or modest clothing is recommended at all temples. Footwear is removed before entering.

What to carry

A valid photo ID, comfortable walking shoes, a water bottle and any regular medication.

Physical activity

Some shrines involve steps or hill climbs. Tell us about mobility needs and we’ll plan accordingly.

Best time to visit

October to March offers pleasant weather. Festival dates may affect crowds and darshan times.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the tour start and end?

The tour starts with a morning flight from Hyderabad to Coimbatore and ends in Chennai.

Can the itinerary be customised?

Yes. We can add or skip temples, change hotel categories, or extend your stay. Share your preferences when you enquire.

Is this tour suitable for senior citizens?

Yes. Travel is by private vehicle and the pace is comfortable. Tell us about any mobility needs in advance.

Are special darshan tickets included?

No, special darshan tickets are not included, but our team can help you book them where available.
