Tour overview

Three of India’s holiest cities in one unhurried journey: Varanasi on the Ganga, Prayagraj where three rivers meet, and Ayodhya, birthplace of Lord Rama.

Watch the evening Ganga Aarti, take a sunrise boat past the ghats, bathe at the Triveni Sangam and offer prayers at the Ram Mandir. A private vehicle, a tour manager, comfortable stays and all meals are included.

Kashi VishwanathThe Jyotirlinga temple and its riverfront corridor in Varanasi
Ganga AartiEvening aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat
Triveni SangamConfluence of the Ganga, Yamuna and the mythical Saraswati
AyodhyaShri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir and Hanuman Garhi

Good to know

Dress code

Traditional or modest clothing is recommended at all temples. Footwear is removed before entering.

What to carry

A valid photo ID, comfortable walking shoes, a water bottle and any regular medication.

Physical activity

Some shrines involve steps or hill climbs. Tell us about mobility needs and we’ll plan accordingly.

Best time to visit

October to March is the most comfortable time. Summers are very hot, and the Magh Mela (January–February) brings large crowds to Prayagraj.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the tour start and end?

The tour starts in Varanasi and ends with a drop-off at Ayodhya airport or Lucknow.

Can the itinerary be customised?

Yes. We can add or skip temples, change hotel categories, or extend your stay. Share your preferences when you enquire.

Is this tour suitable for senior citizens?

Yes. Travel is by private vehicle and the pace is comfortable. Tell us about any mobility needs in advance.

Are special darshan tickets included?

No, special darshan tickets are not included, but our team can help you book them where available.
