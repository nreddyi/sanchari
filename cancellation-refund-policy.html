Cancelling a booking

Please tell us in writing (email or WhatsApp) if you need to cancel. The cancellation date is the day we receive your message.

Cancellation charges

The exact charges for your journey are stated in your booking confirmation. As a guide:

More than 30 days before departure: advance amount is refundable, less non-refundable costs already paid to hotels, airlines or permit offices.

15–30 days before departure: 50% of the tour cost is charged.

Less than 15 days before departure, or no-show: no refund.

If we cancel

If we have to cancel a departure, you can choose another date or receive a full refund of the amount paid to us.

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 7–10 working days.
