FAQs

Questions before you travel

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Call or WhatsApp us on +91 73374 28436.

How fit do I need to be?

Good cardiovascular fitness is essential. We recommend 8–12 weeks of training with long walks, stair climbs and cardio before departure.

Are flights included?

It varies by expedition — the Everest trek includes flights from Hyderabad, while Kilimanjaro and Elbrus do not. Check the inclusions on each tour page.

What happens if the weather turns?

Every itinerary includes buffer or reserve days. Final decisions on summit attempts are made by the expedition leaders for everyone’s safety.

Can I rent equipment?

Yes. A detailed gear list is shared on booking, and personal trekking and mountaineering gear can be rented on request.

Is insurance included?

High-altitude, medical and evacuation cover is included where stated on each tour page. We’ll confirm exactly what is covered when you book.
