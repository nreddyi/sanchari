Tour overview

The Char Dham Yatra is one of Hinduism’s most revered pilgrimages: four shrines high in the Garhwal Himalaya, at the sources of the Yamuna and the Ganga, the Jyotirlinga of Kedarnath and the abode of Lord Vishnu at Badrinath.

This twelve-day journey starts and ends on the Ganga at Haridwar and Rishikesh, with a private vehicle, a tour manager, all meals and stays near each dham. The route is long and mountainous, so we pace it with early starts, rest stops and time to acclimatise.

YamunotriSource of the Yamuna, reached by a trek from Janki Chatti
GangotriTemple of Goddess Ganga on the Bhagirathi river
KedarnathJyotirlinga temple set below snow peaks at 3,583 m
BadrinathShrine of Lord Badri Vishal on the Alaknanda

Good to know

Dress code

Traditional or modest clothing is recommended at all temples. Footwear is removed before entering.

What to carry

A valid photo ID, comfortable walking shoes, a water bottle and any regular medication.

Fitness & altitude

The route reaches 3,583 m at Kedarnath and includes two long treks. A basic fitness routine and a doctor’s check-up are advised; ponies, palkis and helicopters can be arranged.

Best time to visit

The temples open around late April or May and close around October–November; exact dates are announced each year. Avoid the peak monsoon weeks of July and August.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the tour start and end?

The tour starts in Haridwar and ends with a drop-off at Haridwar or Dehradun airport.

Is the yatra suitable for senior citizens?

Yes, with planning. Ponies, palkis and helicopter services can replace the treks. Tell us about any health conditions so we can advise.

Is registration required?

Yes, the Uttarakhand government requires yatra registration for every pilgrim. Our team helps you complete it.

Can I do only Do Dham (Kedarnath & Badrinath)?

Yes. We can plan a shorter Do Dham itinerary of around 6–7 days.
