Tour overview

A classic trek through the Khumbu to Everest Base Camp and the summit of Kala Patthar — passing through rhododendron forests, Sherpa villages and high glacial terrain, with views of Everest, Lhotse, Nuptse and Ama Dablam.

The itinerary includes two acclimatisation days, a visit to Tengboche Monastery and a sunrise climb of Kala Patthar. Experienced guides, porters, meals on the trek and high-altitude insurance are included. The itinerary is indicative and may change with weather and trail conditions.

What’s included

Included

  • Round-trip flights Hyderabad – Kathmandu
  • Airport transfers in Kathmandu
  • 3 nights in 3-star hotels in Kathmandu (B&B)
  • Transfer to Ramechhap & domestic flights to Lukla
  • One porter per two trekkers
  • English-speaking guides (3 per 10 trekkers)
  • All meals on the trek, boiled water & hot drinks
  • First aid, emergency oxygen & communication devices
  • Staff wages & insurance
  • High-altitude, medical & evacuation insurance
  • EBC trek certificate

Not included

  • Personal clothing & trekking equipment
  • Personal expenses (laundry, phone, internet, drinks)
  • Costs from weather changes or unforeseen events
  • Tips for guides & porters
  • Nepal visa fees for non-Indian passport holders

Good to know

Fitness

Good cardiovascular fitness is essential. Start a training plan of walking, stair climbs and cardio at least 8–12 weeks before departure.

Gear

A detailed gear list is shared on booking. Personal trekking and mountaineering gear can be rented on request.

Altitude

The itinerary includes acclimatisation days. Guides monitor every trekker and the plan may change for safety.

Documents

A valid passport, visa where required, and medical and evacuation insurance details are needed before travel.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the expedition start and end?

The trek starts and ends in Kathmandu, with flights from Hyderabad included.

Do I need prior trekking experience?

Prior multi-day trekking experience is recommended. Our team will talk you through fitness preparation before you book.

What happens if the weather turns?

Buffer and reserve days are built in. Final decisions on summit attempts are made by the expedition leaders for everyone’s safety.

Who leads the expedition?

Adventure journeys are run with Anmish Adventures, led by mountaineer Anmish Varma, with experienced local guides and support staff.
