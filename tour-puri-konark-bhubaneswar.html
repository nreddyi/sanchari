Tour overview

Odisha’s golden triangle brings together the temple city of Bhubaneswar, the Jagannath Temple at Puri and the great stone chariot of the Konark Sun Temple.

Explore Kalinga-style temple architecture, the rock-cut caves of Udayagiri and a village of pattachitra painters, with time by the sea at Puri. A private vehicle, a tour manager, stays and all meals are included.

Jagannath TempleOne of the Char Dham shrines, home of Lord Jagannath
Konark13th-century Sun Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage site
LingarajBhubaneswar’s great 11th-century Shiva temple
RaghurajpurHeritage village of pattachitra painters

Good to know

Dress code

Traditional or modest clothing is recommended at all temples. Footwear is removed before entering.

What to carry

A valid photo ID, comfortable walking shoes, a water bottle and any regular medication.

Temple entry

Entry into the Jagannath and Lingaraj temples is restricted to Hindus. Other travellers can view Lingaraj from a platform outside and visit Konark and the other sites.

Best time to visit

October to March is the most comfortable time. The Rath Yatra in Puri (June or July) is spectacular but very crowded.

Frequently asked questions

The tour starts and ends in Bhubaneswar.

No, special darshan tickets are not included, but our team can help you book them where available.
