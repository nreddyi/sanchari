Tour overview

A slower, reflective journey through Kerala’s temple traditions — from Adiyogi in Coimbatore across the Palakkad Gap to Thrissur, Guruvayur and the backwaters, ending at Thiruvananthapuram.

Kerala’s temples have a distinct architecture of sloping roofs, wooden carvings and lamp-lit courtyards. Along the way you’ll spend a night on a houseboat, with all meals, stays and private transport arranged.

GuruvayurOne of the most important Sri Krishna temples in India
VadakkunnathanThrissur’s great Shiva temple at the heart of the city
BackwatersA night aboard a traditional Kerala houseboat

Good to know

Dress code

Traditional or modest clothing is recommended at all temples. Footwear is removed before entering.

What to carry

A valid photo ID, comfortable walking shoes, a water bottle and any regular medication.

Physical activity

Some shrines involve steps or hill climbs. Tell us about mobility needs and we’ll plan accordingly.

Best time to visit

October to March offers pleasant weather. Festival dates may affect crowds and darshan times.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the tour start and end?

The tour starts in Coimbatore in the afternoon of Day 1 and ends in Thiruvananthapuram.

Can the itinerary be customised?

Yes. We can add or skip temples, change hotel categories, or extend your stay. Share your preferences when you enquire.

Is this tour suitable for senior citizens?

Yes. Travel is by private vehicle and the pace is comfortable. Tell us about any mobility needs in advance.

Are special darshan tickets included?

No, special darshan tickets are not included, but our team can help you book them where available.
